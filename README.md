# Tommy's Tracer

![Tommy's Tracer](assets/branding/primary_logo.png)

Make golf videos easier to follow with a clear visual trace of the ball's flight. Tommy's Tracer lets you choose a clip, mark the ball's path, and export a video with the tracer added.

## How to use

1. Open a golf video and choose the section you want to work with by adjusting the **IN** and **OUT** points.
2. Continue to the tracing screen. The app briefly stabilises the footage first (a short progress popup appears and closes on its own), so the tracer follows camera movement.
3. Play or step through the video, placing and adjusting points along the ball's path.
4. Choose the tracer's look and color, then preview your result.
5. Export the finished video, or save your project to continue editing later.

The app is designed for golfers who want to make a shot's flight easier to see and share.
